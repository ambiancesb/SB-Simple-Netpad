Privacy Policy

SB Simple Netpad · Effective July 10, 2026

SB Simple Netpad (“Netpad”) is a peer-to-peer local-network notepad published by Spencer Beaumier. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • Netpad does not operate a cloud service, user accounts, or analytics.
  • Your notes stay on your devices unless you sync them to peers you choose on your LAN.
  • Netpad does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Information stored on your device

Netpad stores the following locally on each device you install it on:

  • Note text, titles, and order
  • Local version-history snapshots
  • Device display name, session/room ID, and app preferences
  • TLS identity (self-signed certificate) and pinned peer certificates
  • Trusted-peer settings, blocklist, and connection log entries

This data is kept in the app’s local storage (for example shared_preferences on mobile and desktop). It is not uploaded to a central server operated by the developer.

Peer-to-peer sync on your local network

When you pair with another Netpad instance on the same local network, the app exchanges note content and related sync metadata directly between devices over encrypted WebSocket connections (wss://). Sync is limited to addresses on your device’s active local subnet. Netpad does not scan the public internet or arbitrary IP ranges.

Peers you accept can receive the notes you sync. You control pairing through mutual approval, trusted-device settings, and block/revoke actions in the app.

Network and discovery

To find other Netpad instances on your LAN, the app may use:

  • mDNS/Bonjour service discovery (_sbnetpad._tcp)
  • Direct connection by IP address on the same subnet (when you enter it)
  • Standard network permissions required by your operating system

On cellular-only mobile devices, peer sync is paused until Wi‑Fi or a personal hotspot is available.

Speech dictation (optional)

On supported mobile platforms, you may dictate into a note using your microphone. Audio is processed through your device’s built-in speech-recognition service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). Netpad does not operate its own speech servers. Those platform providers handle audio according to their own privacy policies.

File actions you initiate

If you use Save, Open, or Share, Netpad interacts with paths and apps you choose through your operating system. The developer does not receive those files.

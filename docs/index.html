SB Simple Netpad

LAN notepad · peer discovery · shared editing

Write plain-text notes on your phone or computer and keep them in sync with other devices on the same Wi‑Fi. Peers discover each other on the local network, pair once with mutual approval, then share notes over encrypted sessions.

Android · iOS · Windows · macOS · Linux

What it does

  • Plain-text editor with line numbers
  • Automatic discovery of nearby Netpad devices on your LAN
  • Mutual Accept before any sync starts
  • Multiple named notes, each syncing independently
  • Multi-peer relay so edits can reach devices through a hub
  • Rooms so separate groups can share one network without mixing
  • Local-only sync — no cloud account for your notes

Free and Standard

Core editing and LAN sync are free (unlimited local notes, up to 3 synced notes and 3 connected peers). Netpad Standard is a one-time unlock for unlimited synced notes and peers, extra skins, version history, trusted auto-sync, and voice dictation — via the App Store, Google Play, or Microsoft Store.

FAQ

Cross-Platform Purchases

SB Simple Netpad is available on multiple app stores, including Google Play, the Microsoft Store, and the Apple App Store.

Each store manages purchases independently. A purchase made on one platform does not automatically unlock Standard features on another platform.

For example:

  • Purchasing the Android version unlocks Standard features on your Android devices using your Google account.
  • Purchasing the Windows version unlocks Standard features on your Windows devices using your Microsoft account.
  • Purchasing the Apple version unlocks Standard features on your Apple devices using your Apple ID.

The free version of SB Simple Netpad remains available on every supported platform. If you use the app on another operating system, you can continue using the free features or choose to purchase the Standard version for that platform.

This approach allows SB Simple Netpad to remain privacy-focused without requiring user accounts, cloud logins, or centralized license tracking.

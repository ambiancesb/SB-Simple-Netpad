SB Simple Netpad

LAN notepad · peer discovery · shared editing

Write plain-text notes on your phone or computer and keep them in sync with other devices on the same Wi‑Fi. Peers discover each other on the local network, pair once with mutual approval, then share notes over encrypted sessions.

Android · iOS · Windows · macOS

What it does

  • Plain-text editor with line numbers
  • Automatic discovery of nearby Netpad devices on your LAN
  • Mutual Accept before any sync starts
  • Multiple named notes, each syncing independently
  • Multi-peer relay so edits can reach devices through a hub
  • Rooms so separate groups can share one network without mixing
  • Local-only sync — no cloud account for your notes

Free and Standard

Core editing and LAN sync are free (unlimited local notes, up to 3 synced notes and 3 connected peers). Netpad Standard is a one-time unlock for unlimited synced notes and peers, extra skins, version history, trusted auto-sync, and voice dictation — via the App Store, Google Play, or Microsoft Store.

FAQ

Does paying for Standard unlock the app?

Yes. On store builds that support in-app purchases (Apple App Store, Google Play, and Microsoft Store), buying Netpad Standard unlocks Standard features immediately on that device. You can also use Restore purchases if you already bought Standard with the same store account.

Standard includes unlimited synced notes and peers, unlimited note length, extra skins, version history, trusted peer auto-sync, and voice dictation.

Cross-platform purchases

Each store manages purchases independently. A purchase on one platform does not automatically unlock Standard on another.

  • Android (Google Play) unlocks Standard on your Android devices with that Google account.
  • Windows (Microsoft Store) unlocks Standard on your Windows devices with that Microsoft account.
  • Apple (App Store / Mac App Store) unlocks Standard on your Apple devices with that Apple ID.

The free version remains available on every supported platform. This keeps Netpad privacy-focused without Netpad accounts, cloud logins, or centralized license tracking.

If you open a store listing from a build where in-app purchases are unavailable (for example a sideloaded build without billing), buying there unlocks Standard on that store’s version of the app.
